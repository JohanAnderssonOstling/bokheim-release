# Review of unrelated LCC crossings

Reviewed all 55 pairs from the original range-hierarchy audit. The findings are:

- 30 pairs affected by confirmed boundary, selector-owner, or parent repairs.
- 19 retained: source scopes overlap and the inspected matches fit their topics.
- 3 flagged for whole-concept consolidation review: social groups/dynamics,
  court/civil procedure, and the two Special Education concepts.
- 2 pairs share one unresolved historical endpoint, `KBM4855.22`. Valid sampled
  codes resolve to Jewish Criminal Law; the printed schedule does not by itself
  establish the extra decimal suffix. It remains unchanged pending historical
  or table evidence.
- 1 offending range had already been removed by other taxonomy curation.

These counts describe the original pair list, not 55 independent defects.
Multiple pairs share the same bad range. The applied
migration repairs
28 existing range rows, adds bounded supporting ranges, moves one exact
selector, and corrects three concepts' ancestry. It also renames the former
Canon Criminal Law concept to Comparative Religious Criminal Law, consistent
with its KB selectors. Matcher algorithms are unchanged.

Concrete corrections include Swiss early-modern history ending at DQ118 rather
than DQ188.2, sixteenth-century French literature ending at PQ1709 rather than
PQ1713, and separate abduction/kidnapping spans that no longer swallow intervening
sex crimes. Patent, trademark, unfair-competition, and copyright codes no longer
resolve to Specialized Courts & Proceedings. Oversized KB, KBM, KIA, and KJC
ranges were checked against actual page text rather than trusting malformed
legacy reference spans. English literary landmarks now have their literary-history
parent instead of essay, letter, and oratory parents.

## Evidence and validation

The local reference contains pinned Library of Congress 2025 PDF page text and
older MARC-derived spans. Some older spans contain the same malformed endpoints
as the taxonomy, so agreement with those spans alone was not accepted as proof.
Source URLs, SHA-256 checksums, and pages are recorded for every pair in the
[review JSON](20260920-unrelated-lcc-crossing-review.json). The original historical
reference records are retained as evidence; reimporting their malformed spans
without these corrections would reintroduce errors.

The production release matcher was compared across **11,223 LCC probes**:
boundaries, synthetic midpoints, whole original range inputs, and active printed
reference codes within the reviewed envelopes. **537 results changed; no
previously resolved probe became unresolved.** All **6,006 BISAC/DDC control
selectors** retained the same candidates and resolved IDs. These probes are not
an exhaustive proof for every possible call-number suffix. Invalid synthetic
numbers may still receive a broad fallback, but no longer the spurious specific
subject generated by an oversized range.

Re-running the geometric audit against matching snapshots immediately before
and after application reduced all crossing pairs from **169 to 141**, and
unrelated crossing pairs from **55 to 26**. This is not a target of zero overlaps:
valid overlapping source scopes remain, including patents versus unfair
competition, which now selects the appropriate topic. Other curation was active
in the workspace, so the original 55-pair list and the immediately-before
snapshot are not assumed to be identical. The migration updates guarded rows
in the current database rather than replacing it with an earlier snapshot.

Six regression tests cover positive matches, adjacent topics, Cutter endpoints,
whole-interval containment, and rejection of erroneous specific assignments.
SQLite integrity and foreign-key checks passed; rerunning the migration produced
an identical database dump. The full release tests also exposed an unrelated
stale label assertion (`Thinking` versus the already-renamed `Thought & Reasoning`);
only that expected label was updated.

Final validation: `cargo test --release -p subject-projection` passed all **278
tests** (259 unit and 19 integration); the separately invoked ignored geometric
audit also passed.
