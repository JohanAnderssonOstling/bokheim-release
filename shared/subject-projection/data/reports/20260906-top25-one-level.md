## Interpretation notes

The date labels follow LCC classification periods; they are not a promise that every edition was published within those dates. French literature, 1961–2000, has no finer topical or period divisions in its base numbers; its biography/criticism child uses the documented P-PZ40 author-table subdivision instead. Cutter-based topic children retain their base selectors and explicit observed descendants for the standalone usage matcher. The French biography/criticism extraction uses observed call numbers; newly imported author-table codes may need additional mappings.
