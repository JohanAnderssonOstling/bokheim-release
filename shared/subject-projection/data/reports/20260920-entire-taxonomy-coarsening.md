# Whole-taxonomy coarsening survey — 2026-09-20

## Scope and status

Screened all 16,502 current subjects across all 24 top-level branches. The inventory flags 346 distinct general/reference/detail buckets or single-child wrappers and 1,186 repeated-label groups. These are screening results, not confirmed duplicates or instructions to merge. No full classification validation was run. No edits from this whole-taxonomy survey were applied.

The separately approved Alcoholism & Temperance compaction was completed first: 18 subjects absorbed into Alcohol Use & Social Effects; Alcohol Rehabilitation and By Region retained. Viewer updated; usage refresh remains deferred.

## Recommended order

1. Science: remove general publication-type clutter and merge the ethics/history overlaps.
2. Film and Education: absorb reference/form leaves, keeping film genres and educational levels.
3. Religion, Technology and Medicine: general-parent compactions, preserving doctrinal and clinical distinctions.
4. Philosophy: repair the incorrect reference scope before further compaction.
5. Remaining branches: review the smaller groups below; preserve useful regional and period groupings.

## Preserve these distinctions

- Children and Young Adult age/audience branches versus adult subject counterparts. Similar labels do not make them duplicates.
- Study Aids: exam-specific categories are useful; no forced coarsening recommendation.
- True Crime: genre subdivisions are useful; no strong compaction found in this pass.
- Personal Growth: preserve practical self-help scope versus academic psychology.
- Reference & Information: dictionaries, encyclopedias and bibliographies are core topics here, not merely disposable book formats.
- Language: Role and Reference Grammar is a named linguistic theory; the word reference is a screening false positive.
- Literature/Arts: literary and humorous essays are genres; do not absorb them just because academic essay buckets elsewhere are coarsened.
- History/Literature: By Region and By Period remain useful even with one child. Uncoded does not mean redundant.
- Travel essays/travelogues and scientific/medical atlases can be meaningful forms; inspect scope before generalizing.
- Two singleton roots, Philosophy, Psychology & Religion and Geography, Anthropology & Recreation, need a separate source-code/root-policy review, not automatic deletion.

## Prison follow-up

Absorb Prison Methods & Practice, U.S.-specific Prison Administration and world-level prison reports into their appropriate broader subjects. Retain Juvenile Offenders, Rehabilitation, Alternatives to Imprisonment, country coverage and Prison Reform. Concentration/Internment Camps and Penal Colonies have historical/legal scopes that should not be folded into ordinary prison administration.
