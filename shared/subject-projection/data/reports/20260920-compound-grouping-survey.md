# Compound grouping survey

Screened 801 headings with children; 361 occur within two edges of a root. Reviewed 18 examples. Report only; no taxonomy changes.

| Heading | Children | Assessment | Review direction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Recreation, Travel & Adventure (5642) | 14 | Placement mismatch | Children’s nonfiction grouping includes Mathematics, Paranormal & Supernatural and Espionage alongside activities and travel. Review outliers and divide coherent branches; retain audience context. |
| Art & Furniture Collectibles (5740) | 14 | Label narrower than contents | Fourteen collectible classes include bottles, buttons, kitchenware and textiles. Retain a substantial grouping, but broaden its name or divide by object type. |
| AI, Data & Emerging Computing (5572) | 9 | Mixed organizing principles | AI, data science and media processing are combined with a changing emerging-computing category. Review separate AI/data and computing-paradigm groupings. |
| IT Applications & Management (5577) | 11 | Mixed organizing principles | Software types, database management, IT auditing and general IT share a heading. Review applications versus IT management scopes. |
| Learning, Reference & Society (5579) | 7 | Catch-all | Learning, reference, careers, history and social aspects are combined. Review separate learning/careers and history/society scopes; check reference code placement. |
| Artists & Art Institutions (5668) | 8 | Label/content mismatch | Artists and institutions share a branch with business, economics and forgeries. Review artist-focused versus art-market/institutional scopes. |
| Family, Population & Gender (5238) | 8 | Broad mixed scope | Demography, migration, geography, family, gender, life stages and lifestyle share one heading. Review internal organization; preserve useful family/gender/life-stage groupings. |
| Optics, Imaging & Acoustics (5569) | 9 | Several disciplines | Optics and imaging sit with acoustics and general sensors. Review distinct optics/imaging and acoustics placement, with sensor scope checked separately. |
| Arts, Media & Story Forms (5635) | 13 | Mixed facets | YA art subjects, literary forms, humor and media tie-ins are grouped together. Review subject versus story-form organization without mixing audiences. |
| History, People, Media & Reference (5901) | 7 | Catch-all | Cooking history, chefs, essays, media tie-ins and collectibles are combined. Review subgroups and potential overlapping history nodes; no equivalence assumed. |
| History, Biography & Community (5873) | 2 | Label broader than direct children | Both direct children are Jewish-history subjects. Check codes and descendants before considering a narrower History of Judaism label. |
| Germanic & Nordic (5587) | 14 | Mixed geographic and linguistic organization | Countries and language labels are combined. Review whether membership follows language, place, or both, preserving useful regional navigation. |
| Uralic & Basque (4479) | 7 | Composite language grouping | Basque is bundled with Uralic-labelled language groups. Review source scope and the existing separate Uralic Languages grouping before proposing any split. |
| Culture, Media & Belief (5662) | 11 | Broad but potentially coherent | Cultural sociology, media, religion, conspiracy theories and secret societies share one substantial branch. Review outliers, not wholesale removal. |
| Wood, Metal, Glass & Ceramics (5686) | 7 | Potentially useful material grouping | Wood, metal, glass and ceramics form a recognizable crafts grouping, though the label is a list. Keep structure unless a better subdivision is demonstrated. |
| People, Society & History (5639) | 8 | Useful broad audience grouping | An eight-child children’s nonfiction society/humanities grouping. Punctuation alone is not a reason to flatten. |
| Fiber, Needlework & Sewing (5683) | 14 | Coherent grouping | Fiber crafts, needlework and sewing are related practices; retain substantial branch. |
| Accounting & Tax (5595) | 3 | Coherent grouping | Accounting, bookkeeping and taxation form a useful business grouping despite only three children. |
