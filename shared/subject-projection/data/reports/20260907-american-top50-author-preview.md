## Method and validation

Candidate discovery combines documented named-author records in the local LCC reference with observed first-author-Cutter usage. High-use unmapped Cutters were checked against primary library catalogs and the Library of Congress bibliography linked above. A 58-candidate staging run measured actual matching behavior around the cutoff before the final 50 were selected. Robert B. Parker makes the final 50 with 395 hits; Janet Dailey and Robert Frost each have 393. This is a ranking among the verified American PS author shelves investigated, not a complete ranking of author identities across all editions, schemes, pseudonyms, and languages.

The final 50-subject database passes 200 selector, work/criticism, neighboring-Cutter, and range-boundary probes. SQLite integrity and foreign-key checks pass. Full before/after matching finds zero classification codes or uses lost and zero newly covered codes; all changed destinations are selected author subjects. Broad era selectors remain as residual coverage.

These are classification-use counts, not unique books. Author shelves include works by and material about their authors. Collaborations, continuations, and works published under an author name can also share the shelf; these selectors do not implement creator attribution. Author nodes are children of the existing American era subjects, following their LCC schedule placement.

The [feasibility report](20260907-american-author-subject-feasibility.md) explains author identity coverage and the existing author-exclusion policy. Reproducible staging scripts, manifests, probes, and verification totals are in `/tmp/american-top50/`; large derived temporary matching files may be regenerated from the retained inputs.
